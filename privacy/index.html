Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28.08.2026

Veriatax Interactive (operated by Sven Martin Laschke, sole proprietor) develops and publishes mobile games and applications. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling data transparently and responsibly.

This privacy policy applies to our mobile applications and games. This website (veriatax.com) serves informational purposes only, shows no advertising, and uses a cookie-free analytics service that does not create user profiles.

This privacy policy is available in German and English. In case of discrepancies, the German version shall prevail.

1. Information We Collect (Mobile Apps)

The following applies to our Android apps only. What happens when you visit this website is described under section 3.

Technical Information

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs
  • Performance diagnostics

Usage Data

  • Session duration
  • Gameplay events
  • Feature interactions
  • App activity

Advertising Data

Our apps use Google AdMob to display advertisements. The following data may be processed:

  • Google Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Approximate location (country/region)
  • Device information

Before any advertising is shown, we ask for your decision through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). There you can consent, decline, or manage individual purposes. Without your consent, only non-personalised advertising is served.

You can change your decision at any time: the form can be reopened from the app's settings. The legal basis for personalised advertising is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Users can reset or disable the Advertising ID and manage ad personalization settings within their Google account or device settings.

Analytics Services

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Remote Config
  • Google Play Games Services

2. Legal Basis (EU Users)

  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent) — for personalised advertising, which you can allow through the consent form on first launch
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest) — for crash reports, technical operation, security, and non-personalised advertising that funds the free app

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. In the apps, data is shared with the following services:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase
  • Google Play Games Services

If you consent to personalised advertising, Google's advertising partners may process data in addition to Google itself. The complete and current list of these partners is available directly in the app's consent form under "List of partners". If you decline, this sharing does not take place.

Further information on how Google processes personal data can be found in Google's Privacy Policy.

External Fonts (Website)

For consistent display of fonts, this website uses Bunny Fonts, a service by BunnyWay d.o.o. (Slovenia, EU). When a page loads, your browser fetches the font files from their servers. As with any external file, your IP address is transmitted to those servers in the process — there is no technical way to deliver the fonts otherwise.

The servers are located within the EU, so no third-country transfer takes place. According to the provider, no cookies are set, no access logs are kept and no user profiles are created. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in consistent and fast presentation).

Hosting & Infrastructure (Website)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered via Cloudflare. When accessing this website, technical data such as IP address, browser type, and referrer URL are automatically processed by these services. This is technically required to ensure the security and reliability of the website (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

To defend against automated abuse and attacks, Cloudflare sets strictly necessary cookies in the process. These are cf_clearance for access verification and __cf_ob from Cloudflare's challenge platform, which distinguishes human visitors from automated traffic. These cookies serve security and reliable operation only. They do not create user profiles and are used neither for advertising nor for cross-site tracking. Further information: overview of Cloudflare cookies.

Analytics (Website)

This website uses GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service, to measure how many people visit which pages. GoatCounter sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and does not track you across websites. It does not build user profiles and collects no data that identifies you personally. Recorded are the page visited, the referring page, approximate country, browser and screen size. IP addresses are used only transiently to derive the country and are not stored. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Further information: GoatCounter Privacy Policy.

4. International Data Transfers

The services we use — Google (apps), plus GitHub and Cloudflare (website) — may process personal data on servers outside the European Union, in particular in the United States.

These transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission adopted an adequacy decision on 10 July 2023, and / or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). The linked list lets you check any provider's certification status at any time.

Bunny Fonts and GoatCounter run on servers within the EU; no third-country transfer takes place there.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described under sections 1 and 3, or as required by statutory retention periods. After that it is deleted or anonymised.

The actual duration depends on the service: crash reports are kept no longer than needed for debugging; technical infrastructure logs follow the retention periods of GitHub and Cloudflare; GoatCounter statistics contain no IP addresses in the first place. As we have no influence over our providers' retention periods, we refer you to their respective privacy notices.

6. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed at children under 13 unless explicitly stated.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights in relation to us:

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR) — what data we process about you
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) — correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) — deletion of your data
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) — for data we process automatically based on your consent or a contract
  • Withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR) — at any time with effect for the future; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand
  • Complaint to a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR) — the authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Rhineland-Palatinate, Germany; you may also contact the authority where you live

An informal email to contact@veriatax.com is enough to exercise these rights.

Right to object under Art. 21 GDPR

Where we process your data on the basis of a legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — which covers the operation of this website, the font delivery and the analytics — you have the right to object to that processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

We will then stop processing the data in question unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms. An informal email is sufficient.

Externe Schriftarten (Website)

Zur einheitlichen Darstellung von Schriftarten nutzt diese Website Bunny Fonts, einen Dienst der BunnyWay d.o.o. (Slowenien, EU). Beim Laden einer Seite ruft Ihr Browser die Schriftdateien von deren Servern ab. Wie bei jedem Abruf einer externen Datei wird dabei Ihre IP-Adresse an diese Server übertragen — technisch anders lassen sich die Schriften nicht ausliefern.

Die Server stehen innerhalb der EU, eine Übermittlung in ein Drittland findet also nicht statt. Nach Angaben des Anbieters werden dabei keine Cookies gesetzt, keine Zugriffsprotokolle geführt und keine Nutzerprofile gebildet. Hosting & Infrastruktur (Website)

Diese Website wird über GitHub Pages gehostet und über Cloudflare ausgeliefert. Beim Aufruf dieser Website werden technische Daten wie IP-Adresse, Browsertyp und Referrer-URL automatisch durch diese Dienste verarbeitet. Dies ist technisch erforderlich, um die Sicherheit und Stabilität der Website zu gewährleisten (Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO).

Zur Abwehr von automatisiertem Missbrauch und Angriffen setzt Cloudflare dabei technisch notwendige Cookies. Dazu gehören cf_clearance zur Zugriffsprüfung sowie __cf_ob aus der Challenge-Plattform von Cloudflare, das der Unterscheidung zwischen menschlichen Besuchern und automatisiertem Datenverkehr dient. Diese Cookies dienen ausschließlich der Sicherheit und dem störungsfreien Betrieb der Website. Sie erstellen keine Nutzerprofile und werden weder für Werbung noch für websitesübergreifendes Tracking verwendet. Weitere Informationen: Übersicht der Cloudflare-Cookies.

Reichweitenmessung (Website)

Diese Website nutzt GoatCounter, einen datenschutzfreundlichen Analysedienst, um zu messen, wie viele Personen welche Seiten aufrufen. GoatCounter setzt keine Cookies, speichert nichts auf Ihrem Gerät und verfolgt Sie nicht über Websites hinweg. Es werden keine Nutzerprofile gebildet und keine Daten erhoben, die Sie persönlich identifizieren. Erfasst werden die aufgerufene Seite, die verweisende Seite, das ungefähre Land, Browser und Bildschirmgröße. IP-Adressen werden nur kurzzeitig zur Landesbestimmung verwendet und nicht gespeichert. Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO. Weitere Informationen: Datenschutzerklärung von GoatCounter.
