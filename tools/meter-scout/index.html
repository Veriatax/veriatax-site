Meter Scout

Will your payments cover your bill?

Meter readings & bill check

Enter your meter readings, tariff and agreed monthly payments. Meter Scout estimates the total, shows how much you may owe or get back, and suggests an adjustment to your remaining payments.

In Development Android Free + one-time Pro Works offline No account, no ads German & English
Coming Soon on Google Play

Currently in testing. Free version: up to three meters at one property.

Screenshots

💶

Bill check

Estimated costs for the billing period, the expected amount due or credit, and how much to adjust your remaining payments. Clearly marked as an estimate, not a bill.

⚡

Electricity, gas, water, heat

Record any of them. Gas can be shown in m³ or in kWh using your calorific value and conversion factor.

📊

Usage history

Follow your consumption by day, month or year.

🔔

Reading reminder

An optional monthly reminder, scheduled as a local notification on your phone.

💾

Backup & export

Back up all data as a JSON file or export readings as CSV, and choose where they go via Android’s share menu.

🔒

No account, no ads, no tracking

Your data is stored on your device. How the app handles it is explained in the privacy policy.

⭐

Pro as a one-time purchase

Check all your meters, manage several properties, use dual-rate electricity meters, see estimated solar export earnings and add the home screen widget. No subscription.

🌙

Light or dark

Light, dark or matching your device. Pro adds two more colour themes.

🌍

German & English

The app follows your phone’s language, or you choose one in the settings.

Meter Scout is still in testing and will be available on Google Play soon.
