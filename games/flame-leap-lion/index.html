Circus Run

One-Tap Arcade Game

Tap to make your circus lion leap through blazing fire rings — the better your timing, the higher your score! Easy to learn but tough to master, every run is a new chance to beat your high score.

Screenshots

🔥

One-Tap Controls

Tap to leap through the fire rings. Aim for the centre, build combos and watch your score soar. Three difficulty levels: Easy, Normal and Hard.

🦁

6 Characters

Leo the Lion, Tara the Tiger, Ziggy the Zebra, Shadow the Panther, Frost the White Tiger and Blaze the Phoenix. Collect coins and unlock them in the shop.

💥

Power-Ups & Boss Rings

Shield, Slow, Magnet, Mini, Giant and Rescue Bounce. Boss rings block the way, and the rainbow bonus pays out extra points.

📅

Daily Reward & Challenges

A daily reward with a seven-day streak, plus daily challenges that earn you extra coins.

🏆

Leaderboards & Achievements

Four leaderboards via Google Play Games, one per difficulty plus best combo, and 27 achievements to unlock.

📊

Statistics

Games played, rings passed, jumps, coins collected and your best combo at a glance.

🌍

German & English

The whole game is available in German and English.

🎵

Original Soundtrack

An instrumental circus-themed soundtrack composed specifically for the game — no vocals, made for the big top rather than the playlist.

⚡

Smooth Performance

Built with React + Capacitor for buttery smooth 60fps on modern Android devices. Quick sessions, perfect for short breaks.

Free to play, no account required — just tap and leap.
