MindForge

Train Your Brain

Puzzle Collection — Sudoku, Crossmath & Crossword

Three puzzle types in one app, each with its own level progression and three difficulty settings. Plus a daily challenge, achievements and Google Play leaderboards.

In Development Android Free to Play 3 Puzzle Modes 16 Designs German & English
Coming Soon on Google Play

Screenshots

🔢

Sudoku

Classic 9×9 grids with notes, auto-notes, undo and redo. Three lives per round — every mistake counts.

➕

Crossmath

Arithmetic instead of letters: fill in the missing numbers so every equation across and down works out.

📝

Crossword

Clues sit directly in the grid, with an on-screen keyboard built for one-handed solving.

📅

Three Daily Puzzles

One Sudoku, one Crossmath and one Crossword every day — the same for everyone. With a streak counter, best-time leaderboards and an archive of past days.

🎨

16 Designs

Eight colour themes and eight special designs with a background motif, such as Aurora, Deep Sea or Forge Fire. Four of them can also be unlocked for free through play.

🧘

Zen Mode

No timer, no lives — just relax. Available on every difficulty level.

🏆

Leaderboards & Achievements

33 achievements and seven Google Play leaderboards: puzzles solved, solved with hearts, hard puzzles, daily streak and the best daily time for each puzzle type.

🪙

Coins & Hints

Earn coins from puzzles, daily rewards and achievements, and spend them on hints or new designs. Prefer no ads? A one-time purchase makes the game ad-free forever.

🌍

German & English

The whole app runs in either language — menus, clues and puzzle names included. Crossmath is called Mathe-Rätsel on the German side.
