Sudoku

Classic 9×9 grids with a notes mode, undo and redo. Three lives per round — every mistake counts.

Crossmath

Arithmetic instead of letters: place numbers and operators so every row and column adds up.

📝

Crossword

Clues sit directly in the grid, with an on-screen keyboard built for one-handed solving.

Daily Challenge

One new puzzle every day — the same one for everyone, with its own leaderboard and streak counter.

Leaderboards & Achievements

Google Play Games integration: puzzles solved, hardcore ranking, daily streak and best times per mode.

🪙

Coins & Hints

Earn coins from achievements and spend them on hints when a puzzle refuses to budge.

German & English
